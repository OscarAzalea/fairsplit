# FairSplit user test guide

Use this 10-minute session with a creative collaborator or small-team lead. Ask for permission to take notes. Do not present the example team or its financial history as real.

## Tasks

1. Ask the participant to find the current revenue split and explain what would happen if a new inflow arrived today.
2. Ask them to change one share, review the AI mediation result, and explain what they think the AI is recommending.
3. Ask them to identify who must approve a new agreement and what becomes part of the ledger.
4. Ask them to run checkout in the configured Sandbox or simulator and identify which mode was used and which amount was captured.
5. Ask them to find one collaborator's pending balance and explain how it was calculated.

## Questions

- Which screen or number was hardest to understand?
- Did the AI result help the team discuss a disagreement? What would you change?
- What information would you need before trusting a payout recommendation?
- Would you use this for a real project? What would stop you?
- Who should be allowed to change splits, approve agreements, add collaborators, and send money?

Record observations and quotes only with the participant's permission. Do not describe this guide as completed research until real sessions have been held.
